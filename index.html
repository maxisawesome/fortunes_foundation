How to Play

  • The deck — 74 cards: four suits of 13 (Ace–King) plus the 22 major arcana (0–21), which act as a fifth suit.
  • The goal — collect every card onto the foundations at the top. Suits build upward from their Aces (already placed). The major arcana build from both ends: up from 0 and down from 21, meeting in the middle.
  • The tableau — a card may be placed on a same-suit card exactly one rank higher or lower. Only one card moves at a time. An empty column takes any card.
  • The free cell — the center slot holds one card, but while it's occupied, suit cards cannot be collected. Use it to keep cards you still need from flying to the foundations.
  • Moving — click a card to select it (legal spots glow green), then click a destination. Double-click sends a card to a foundation, or to the free cell.
  • Undo — U or Cmd+Z. No shame in it; even the original shipped with one.
